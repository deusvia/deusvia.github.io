Privacy Policy — Deus Studio Uploader

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What this app is

Deus Studio Uploader is a private tool run by a single creator to upload and schedule videos on that creator's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

Google data the app accesses

  • Basic YouTube channel information (channel ID and title), to confirm the right channel before uploading.
  • Permission to upload videos and manage the creator's own videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the creator's own videos and to check that an upload succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer removes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
