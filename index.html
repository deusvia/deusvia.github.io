Deus Studio Uploader

Deus Studio Uploader is a private tool used by one creator to upload and schedule videos on their own YouTube channels (for example AhaThings) through the YouTube Data API.

It signs in with Google OAuth, uploads a video file as private with a scheduled publish time, and sets the video's title, description, thumbnail, captions and playlist. It only acts on channels owned by the signed-in user.

It is not a public service and does not collect data from other people.
